Maze Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Maze Rush handles information when you play.

Game data

The app stores profile name, avatar, level progress, scores, achievements, play times, daily results, and settings locally on your device.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games, Google may use your Play Games identity, display name, and Saved Games to synchronize or restore progress. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising and consent

Maze Rush uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad requests or interactions for advertising, measurement, consent, and fraud prevention. Where required, consent is requested before personalized ads. Privacy choices may be reopened from the app Settings screen.

How data is used

Information is used to provide gameplay and progression, restore saves through Play Games, display and measure advertising, manage consent, and protect services from misuse. The developer does not operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Storage, choices, and deletion

Local data remains on the device until removed or deleted. Device backup features may retain a copy. In Settings, you can delete local progress and, when available, Play Games cloud saves. Uninstalling removes active local app data. Google retains information it collects according to Google's policies.

Children

Maze Rush is not directed to children under 13, or the higher age required by local law. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

International processing and security

Google services may process data internationally. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will remain available at this URL.
